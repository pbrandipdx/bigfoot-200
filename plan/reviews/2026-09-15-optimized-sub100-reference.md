# Bigfoot 200 — Optimized Sub-100 Training Reference

**Patrick Brandimore** · Optimized Version · Target Race: **August 13, 2027**

> **Goal:** Finish Bigfoot 200 under **100:00** (Western States 100 Qualifier Standard)
> **Race Course:** 200.1 mi · 44,082 ft gain · 45,563 ft loss · 107-hour official cutoff
> **Target Schedule:** **97:00** (3-hour safety buffer)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Adjustments

This optimized plan aligns your training with published ultra-coaching consensus (Jason Koop, David Roche/SWAP, Camille Herron, Adam Holz, Candice Burt) while correcting baseline discrepancies from early plan drafts.

### Critical Plan Adjustments:
1. **Rescaled Baseline:** Plan targets now start from your measured baseline (**8.8 hr/wk**, **1,700 ft vert/wk**, **29 mi/wk**) rather than an overstated 12 hr / 3,000 ft base.
2. **Capped Peak Volume:** Peak weekly volume is capped at **14–16 hr/wk** (down from 22–24 hr/wk), maintaining consistency without triggering overtraining syndrome (OTS).
3. **Eliminated the 20-Hour Single Training Run:** The single long run is capped at **7–8 hours max**. Single runs over 8 hours produce high musculoskeletal damage and recovery debt with diminishing aerobic returns. Long-distance fatigue is trained via **back-to-back weekend runs** (Sat 6h + Sun 3-4h).
4. **Enforced 3:1 Microcycle Periodization:** Every 4th week is a mandatory **deload week** (60–70% of block volume) across all 5 blocks.
5. **Aid Station & Logistics Discipline:** Sub-100 hours is an aid station efficiency problem. Non-moving time is capped at **10h 12m total** (6h sleep across 2 stations + 4h12m across 12 aid stops = ~21 min avg per stop).
6. **Bighorn 100 Re-Framing:** Bighorn 100 (8 weeks before Bigfoot) is run strictly at Zone 1 / low intensity (target 28–30 hrs) as a gear/nutrition rehearsal, preserving quad freshness for Block 5.

## 3. Weekly Workout Menu & Tactical Upgrades

### Monday: Mandatory Rest & Recovery
* **Goal:** Full systemic recovery, reset HRV balance. Zero running.

### Tuesday: Uphill Power & Aerobic Capacity
* **Workout:** 20 min Z1 warm-up + Uphill Tempo Intervals (Wk 1: 3×5m Z3 → Wk 4: 2×15m Z3) + 5×20s Power Hill Strides @ 85% grade effort. 20 min cool-down.

### Wednesday: Mid-Week Aerobic + Heavy Eccentric Strength
* **Run:** 60–75 min Zone 2 trail run.
* **Strength Focus (30 min):** Single-leg box step-downs (3×10), Weighted step-ups with pack (3×12), Dumbbell single-leg RDLs (3×10), Loaded pack carries.

### Thursday: Sustained Downhill Quad Armor
* **Workout:** 60–90 min trail run featuring **10–30 minutes of continuous downhill running**. Focus on light, high-cadence foot strike to build eccentric quad resistance for Bigfoot's 45,563 ft of loss.

### Friday: Easy Recovery Walk / Jog
* **Workout:** 30–45 min Zone 1 active recovery.

### Saturday: Key Long Run (Time-on-Feet)
* **Workout:** 4 to 8 hours Zone 2 mountain trail run/hike. Practicing **70–90g carbohydrate per hour** and gear carrying.

### Sunday: Back-to-Back Long Hike/Run
* **Workout:** 2.5 to 4 hours Zone 2 trail hike/run with race pack. Simulates moving on tired legs without the joint destruction of a single 12+ hour day.

## 5. Safeguards & HRV Monitors

* **ACWR (Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio):** Keep between 0.8 and 1.3. If ACWR > 1.45, cut weekend long run by 30%.
* **HRV Status:** If Garmin HRV Status is unbalanced for 4+ consecutive days, convert all quality workouts to Zone 1 walks until balanced.
* **Descent HR Gap:** Heart rate on descents should remain **≥15 bpm lower** than climb HR. A narrowing gap signals quad fatigue or dehydration.
